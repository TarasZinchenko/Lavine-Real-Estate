# Target Customer Analysis — Papa-Realtor

> **Objective:** Define the target audience for Kharkiv realtor listings, identifying key visual and structural expectations, constraints, and trust signals to guide all UX, copy, and technical design decisions.

---

## 1. Demographic & Platform Context

- **Target Persona:** Kharkiv region apartment buyers and renters.
- **Age Bracket:** 25 to 55 years old (first-time buyers, families upsizing, and independent investors).
- **Primary Source:** Users coming from **Instagram stories/reels** or direct messages sent by the realtor on **Telegram, Viber, or WhatsApp**.
- **Tech Literacy:** Daily users of mobile chat and social media apps. They are comfortable with smartphones but expect zero friction. Any complexity (e.g. complex navigation menus, slow-loading assets) will cause them to exit immediately.
- **Primary Device:** **Mobile (70% - 85% of traffic)**. Many users will view listings on 3G/4G mobile data connections while commuting or walking, making asset optimization and fast load times paramount.

---

## 2. The 10-Second Information Priority

When a prospective buyer opens a shared link, they must see the following details above the fold or within a single scroll:

1. **Building Name & Exact Address:** Proximity to landmarks or metro stations (e.g., Kholodna Hora) matters significantly in Kharkiv.
2. **Clear Price (in USD):** Real estate in Ukraine is historically priced in US dollars. Showing UAH only or hides price under "on request" breeds suspicion for economy-class complexes.
3. **Key Stats:** Floor, total area ($m^2$), and number of rooms.
4. **Before/After Renovation Slider:** Visual proof of potential. Buyers of "строительное состояние" (bare shell) apartments need help visualizing the final product.

---

## 3. Trust Builders & Trust Killers in Kharkiv Real Estate

### Trust Builders:
- **Visual Contrast (The Slide):** Seeing the raw concrete/brick state ("before") side-by-side with a realistic, perspective-locked design project ("after") builds confidence that the design fits the actual room.
- **Clear Construction Type:** Mentioning that a building like ЖК Сказка is constructed of solid brick (rather than prefabricated panels) is a major quality indicator.
- **Realtor Identity:** A prominent, professional photo of the realtor with instant one-tap buttons to call or message.

### Trust Killers:
- **Perspective Misalignment:** Sliders where the room geometry or window views change completely between "before" and "after" make the listing look like generic, deceptive stock rendering.
- **Slow Loading:** A white screen or spinner loading for more than 4 seconds on mobile data leads to immediate abandonment.
- **Generic/Broken UI:** Small hit targets on mobile, or buttons that lead to broken links.

---
